What do you charge, and when do I pay?您怎么收费？什么时候付？

I charge a service fee for representing you — most Bay Area rentals pay nothing to a tenant's agent, so this is how the work gets paid for. The amount depends on the home and how much you need: a full search with showings and application support is priced differently from help with one home you already found. I tell you the number before we start, put it in writing, and nothing is due until you have a signed lease. Separately, some owners charge their own application or screening fee — that is paid to them, not to me.代表您找房我会收取服务费。湾区大多数出租房源并不向租客经纪支付佣金，所以这部分工作由服务费来支付。具体金额取决于房屋情况和您需要的服务范围：完整找房（含带看和申请协助）与「只帮您看一套已经找到的房子」收费不同。开始之前我就会告诉您具体数字并书面确认，在您拿到签好的租约之前不需要支付任何费用。另外，有些屋主会收取他们自己的申请费或审核费 — 那是付给屋主的，不是付给我的。

How much income do I need?我需要多少收入？

Most Bay Area owners look for household income around 3× the monthly rent, counting everyone on the lease. Some accept 2.5× with strong savings or a co-signer. Use the calculator above for your numbers — and if you're close, tell me rather than guessing.湾区大多数屋主要求家庭收入约为月租金的 3 倍，按租约上所有人合计。有的屋主在存款充足或有担保人的情况下接受 2.5 倍。上面的计算器可以看您的数字 — 如果接近，直接告诉我，不用自己猜。

I have no U.S. credit history. Can I still rent?我没有美国信用记录，还能租到房吗？

Often yes. Bank statements, proof of savings, an employment letter or signed offer, a co-signer, or a larger deposit where the owner allows it can all stand in. The key is submitting the substitutes up front with an explanation, not leaving the credit line blank.通常可以。银行对账单、存款证明、在职证明或正式录用信、担保人，或在屋主同意时提高押金，都可以作为替代材料。关键是一开始就连同说明一起提交，而不是让信用那一栏空着。

How much cash do I need at move-in?入住时需要准备多少现金？

Typically the first month's rent plus a security deposit, so roughly two months' rent. California limits how much security deposit most owners can collect for residential leases. Ask for the exact figure in writing before you commit.通常是首月租金加押金，大约相当于两个月租金。加州法律对多数住宅租约的押金上限有限制。在做出承诺之前，请要求屋主书面说明确切金额。

Can you show me a home I found myself?我自己找到的房子，您能带看吗？

What should I never do when renting here?在这里租房，有什么绝对不能做？
