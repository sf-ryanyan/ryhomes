How long will it take to rent my home?我的房子多久能租出去？

It depends on the market, the price, and the condition of the home. My featured homes leased in 7 days on average, and the fastest on record was 2 days — but I won't promise you a number before I've seen your property and the comparable leases near it.取决于市场、定价和房屋状况。我的精选案例平均 7 天租出，最快纪录是 2 天 — 但在看过您的房子和附近成交租约之前，我不会给您一个承诺数字。

What does it cost, and when do I pay?收费多少？什么时候付？

The rental analysis is free and there is no upfront fee. Leasing and management fees are quoted per home based on the property, the city and the service level, in writing, before you sign anything.出租评估免费，前期不收费。出租与管理费用会根据房屋、地区和服务内容单独书面报价，在您签署任何文件之前告知。

How do you screen tenants?您怎么审核租客？

Credit report, income and employment verification, rental history, and a prior-eviction check — the same written standard applied to every applicant, as Fair Housing law requires. You see a written summary of each verified application and you make the final choice.信用报告、收入与在职核实、租房历史、驱逐记录查询 — 所有申请人适用同一套书面标准，符合公平住房法要求。每份核实后的申请都会附书面摘要给您，最终由您决定。

Do I have to repaint or renovate first?出租前一定要重新粉刷或装修吗？

Usually not. After the walkthrough I'll give you a short list split into two: what genuinely raises the rent or speeds up the lease, and what is a waste of money. Clean, working, and well-lit beats newly renovated more often than owners expect.通常不需要。看房之后我会给您一份清单，分成两类：真正能提高租金或加快出租的，以及纯属浪费钱的。干净、设备正常、采光好，往往比重新装修更有效。

What if the tenant stops paying or damages the home?如果租客不付租金或损坏房屋怎么办？

This is exactly why screening and the move-in condition record matter. If rent is late I follow up immediately and keep a written record from day one.
