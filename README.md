## 🔑 For renters ｜ 租客服务

In this market the hard part isn't finding a listing — it's being the application the owner says yes to.

在这个市场，难的不是找到房源，而是成为屋主点头的那份申请。

| Service | 服务 |
|---|---|
| An honest read on what your budget gets in each city | 坦白告诉您这个预算在各城市能租到什么 |
| Your nine-document package built once, reused for every application | 九项材料一次备齐，每次申请都能复用 |
| Showings in person or by video, evenings and weekends | 现场或视频带看，晚间周末都可以 |
| An honest read on your approval odds before you pay a screening fee | 在您付审核费之前，坦白评估通过可能性 |
| The lease explained line by line before you sign | 签约前租约逐条讲解 |
| Dated move-in condition record — your protection at move-out | 带日期的入住记录 —— 退租时的保障 |

**Special situations I handle often ｜ 我经常处理的四种情况**

New job with no local pay stubs · Students and interns · New to the U.S. with thin or no credit · Visa holders and relocating families

刚入职没有本地工资单 · 学生与实习生 · 刚来美国信用记录薄 · 签证持有人与跨城搬迁家庭

> This is tenant representation. My service fee depends on the home and how much you need — I tell you the number in our first conversation, put it in writing, and nothing is due until you have a signed lease. Some owners charge their own application or screening fee; that is paid to them, not to me.<br>
> 这是租客代理服务。服务费按房屋情况和服务范围而定 —— 第一次沟通就告诉您具体数字并书面确认，在您拿到签好的租约之前不需要支付任何费用。部分屋主会收取自己的申请费或审核费，那是付给屋主的，不是付给我的。
